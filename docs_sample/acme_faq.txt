ACME SaaS Platform — Customer Support FAQ
==========================================

SECTION 1: BILLING & SUBSCRIPTIONS
------------------------------------

Q: How do I upgrade my plan?
A: Go to Settings → Billing → Upgrade Plan. Choose from Starter ($29/mo), Pro ($79/mo), or Enterprise (custom). Changes take effect immediately and are prorated.

Q: What is the refund policy?
A: We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for new customers. After 14 days, refunds are not available for the current billing period, but you can cancel anytime to avoid future charges.

Q: How do I update my payment method?
A: Go to Settings → Billing → Payment Methods. Click "Add New Card" and enter your card details. You can set any card as the default.

Q: What happens if my payment fails?
A: We retry the payment 3 times over 7 days. If all retries fail, your account is downgraded to the free tier. Your data is preserved for 30 days.


SECTION 2: ACCOUNT & LOGIN
----------------------------

Q: I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
A: On the login page, click "Forgot Password". Enter your email and we'll send a reset link valid for 24 hours.

Q: How do I enable two-factor authentication (2FA)?
A: Go to Settings → Security → Enable 2FA. We support Google Authenticator and SMS-based OTP.

Q: Can I change my email address?
A: Yes. Go to Settings → Profile → Edit Email. You'll need to verify the new email before the change takes effect.

Q: How do I delete my account?
A: Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. This is irreversible. All your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days.


SECTION 3: TECHNICAL ISSUES
------------------------------

Q: The app is not loading. What should I do?
A: Try these steps: (1) Clear browser cache and cookies. (2) Try a different browser. (3) Disable browser extensions. (4) Check our status page at status.acme.com.

Q: I'm getting a 403 Forbidden error.
A: This usually means your session has expired. Log out and log back in. If the issue persists, your account may have been suspended — contact support.

Q: How do I export my data?
A: Go to Settings → Data → Export. You can export all your data as a CSV or JSON file. Large exports may take up to 10 minutes.

Q: What are the API rate limits?
A: Starter: 100 requests/min. Pro: 1000 requests/min. Enterprise: unlimited. If you exceed limits, you'll receive a 429 Too Many Requests response.


SECTION 4: INTEGRATIONS
--------------------------

Q: Does ACME integrate with Slack?
A: Yes. Go to Integrations → Slack → Connect. You'll need to authorize ACME in your Slack workspace. You can receive notifications and run commands from Slack.

Q: How do I connect to Zapier?
A: Go to Integrations → Zapier → Copy API Key. Use this key in your Zap. We support 50+ triggers and actions.

Q: Is there a REST API?
A: Yes. Documentation is available at docs.acme.com/api. All endpoints require Bearer token authentication. You can generate tokens under Settings → API Keys.


SECTION 5: SECURITY & COMPLIANCE
-----------------------------------

Q: Is my data encrypted?
A: Yes. All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). We are SOC 2 Type II and GDPR compliant.

Q: Where is my data stored?
A: Data is stored in AWS us-east-1 by default. Enterprise customers can request EU or APAC regions.

Q: Do you offer SSO?
A: Yes, SSO via SAML 2.0 and OAuth 2.0 is available on the Enterprise plan. Contact sales@acme.com to set this up.
